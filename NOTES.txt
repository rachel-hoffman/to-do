Versions
V1 - Items are dictionaries {'Title':Bool} where Bool is the checked/unchecked state.
V2 - Rewritten to have an item class, and all items are an instance of that class. Class instances are deconstructed, written to file, and then recreated for use.
V3 - Restructured to have two screens through PySide logic. MainWindow houses the titlebar and that is it.
V4 - Currently in progress.

STATUS
V3 is .exe on my computer, awaiting updates.
